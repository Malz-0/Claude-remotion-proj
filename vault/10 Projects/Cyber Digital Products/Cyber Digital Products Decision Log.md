## B-003 · UK eligibility check before selling — 2026-11-01 (open)
- **Student visa holders cannot be self-employed** (gov.uk/student-visa). If on a Student visa → don't trade; build the portfolio only.
- Home/settled students: first £1,000/yr of trading income is covered by the **trading allowance**; above that,
  register for Self Assessment (gov.uk/income-tax, gov.uk/student-jobs-paying-tax).
