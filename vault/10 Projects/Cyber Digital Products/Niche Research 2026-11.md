## Why #1
- Buyers are **businesses**, with less price sensitivity than students and a clear trigger: insurers and supply-chain contracts are asking for Cyber Essentials.
- Gap in awareness, not just in tooling: most micro businesses don't know where to start.
- **Must beat free:** NCSC publishes free guidance [^ncsc]. Value = done-for-you editable policies,
  a plain-English checklist mapped to the 5 controls, a pre-assessment questionnaire walk-through and short explainer videos.

## Risks / rules
- Don't imply NCSC/IASME endorsement or guarantee certification; "Cyber Essentials" is a government scheme name, so use it descriptively only.
- Security+: never use exam "dumps" (breaches CompTIA's candidate agreement); "CompTIA" and "Security+" are trademarks, so use them descriptively only.
- AI-generated content: review every item yourself; marketplaces penalise low-quality AI listings.
