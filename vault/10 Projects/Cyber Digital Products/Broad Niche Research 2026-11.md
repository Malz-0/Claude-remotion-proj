## Shortlist
1. **GCSE / A-level revision packs (UK)** — high, stable demand; Etsy top results have few reviews;
   seasonal (Jan mocks → Apr–May exams). Start with one subject you know (e.g. GCSE Computer Science).
   Rules: write original content; no copied past papers or mark schemes; name exam boards descriptively.
   Great fit for short revision videos with [[Remotion]] (TikTok/Shorts).
2. **UK money spreadsheets** — budget interest +72%; incumbents are American. UK-specific angle:
   monthly pay, council tax, student loan, ISA, UK tax year, cost-of-living trackers. Low price → bundles.
3. **Industry-specific Canva template packs** — fastest growth (+198%) but saturated; only with a sharp
   niche (e.g. UK tradespeople, salons) and original design.
- Keep: the Cyber Essentials kit as a premium B2B product ([[Niche Research 2026-11]]).
- Avoid for now: CV templates (declining), wedding templates (declining), generic planners/AI prompt packs (flooded).
